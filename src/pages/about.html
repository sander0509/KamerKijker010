Our story

We got tired of hearing about friends losing their deposits.

We’re Sander, Salim, Nick and Levi, students from Rotterdam. Kamerkijker 010 is our student company: we check rooms for people who can’t be here yet. In the busy months we work with a small group of trained student checkers.

  • IndependentWe only work for the tenant and never take money from landlords.
  • HonestWe tell you what we can and can’t check. No false guarantees.
  • LocalReal Rotterdam students who know the city and the tricks.
