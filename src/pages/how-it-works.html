How it works

From ad to answer in 48 hours.

Scroll through the five steps. The phone shows exactly what you see on your own screen, from booking to the final report.

Step 1Hour 0 · 2 minutes

Send us the ad.

Paste the link to the ad, add the address and the landlord’s contact details, and pay securely online.

  • Any site: Facebook groups, Kamernet, Pararius, WhatsApp
  • Pay with iDEAL, card, Apple Pay or Google Pay
  • Parents can book for their child
Step 2Within 24 hours

We check online.

We search the photos, check the official owner in the Kadaster (the Dutch Land Registry), look at the bank account country and compare with our scam radar.

  • Red flag? You hear it straight away
  • And you don’t pay for the visit
Step 3Within 48 hours

We go and look.

One of us makes an appointment with the landlord and visits the room. We film the room, the entrance, the stairwell and the street, and check the key and access.

  • Visits during the day or in pairs
  • We never film housemates without permission
Step 4Same day as the visit

You get the report.

A push message and an email: your report is ready. It opens with the video tour, with our checklist next to it, followed by the photos and the owner and money checks.

  • Video of the real room, not the ad photos
  • Your parents get a copy too
Step 5Your call

You decide.

The report ends with a clear advice: green, orange or red, with the reason. We don’t negotiate and we don’t sign anything for you. We make sure you know what you’re paying for.
