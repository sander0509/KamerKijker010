The problem

Room scams in the Netherlands are rising.

Scammers ask for a deposit and the first month’s rent up front, through Western Union, MoneyGram or a foreign bank account. They are almost never punished.

11%

of reports to the national student union’s Housing Hotline in 2025 were about scams. A few years earlier, it was 2%.

Source: LSVb Housing Hotline report 2025
€1,000–1,500

What victims in Rotterdam typically lose: deposit plus first rent, gone.

Source: Erasmus Magazine
