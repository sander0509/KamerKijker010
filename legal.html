Disclaimer

Kamerkijker 010 performs a visual and administrative check based on a fixed checklist, on the date of the visit. We do not guarantee that a landlord, room or contract is legitimate, and we do not provide legal advice. We work only on behalf of the tenant and receive no payment from landlords. Final decisions remain the responsibility of the client.

Terms in short

  1. The job: we carry out a careful check following our checklist. We don’t promise a result.
  2. Scope: our website explains what we do and don’t check. Our report always says what we couldn’t verify.
  3. Cancellation and refunds: a full refund if we haven’t started. A partial refund if we can’t get access to the room.
  4. Liability: our liability is limited to a reasonable amount. We have professional liability insurance.
  5. Independence: we never accept payment from landlords.
  6. Right of withdrawal: you ask us to start right away. Your right of withdrawal ends once the service has been fully delivered.

Privacy

  • We only use your data for your check and delete it after 6 months.
  • Owner details from the Kadaster are only used for your check and never appear on the website.
  • We never film residents without their permission.
  • The scam radar never contains personal data: only an area, a pattern, a date and a status.
  • We use privacy-friendly analytics without tracking cookies.

Team safety

Visits happen during the day or in pairs, and we share our location. If we don’t feel safe, we don’t go in, and you get a refund.
